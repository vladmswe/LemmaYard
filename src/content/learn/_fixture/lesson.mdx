## Why it exists

This page is the content pipeline's own test. It uses every lesson component once,
so a broken component shows up here before it reaches a real lesson. A stack is a
<Term id="fixture-lifo">LIFO</Term> structure, and the <Term id="fixture-lifo">LIFO</Term> rule
decides what pop returns.

## Mental model
